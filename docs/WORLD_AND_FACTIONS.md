# THE HOLLOW BENEATH — World & Factions

*A lore and setting reference for the descent. Five chapters stand between the sunlit surface and the thing that waits at the bottom.*

---

## 1. The Premise

Five thousand years ago, a civilization called the **Venn** walked into the dark beneath their cities and did not return. They did not die, flee, or fall — they *left*: deliberately, systematically, through doors that should not exist, into something they called the **Loom**.

**Lyra Vane**, linguist and expedition guide, is three days into the **Keth-7 survey** — the latest in a century of attempts to map the Venn's subterranean ruins. A cave-in at the Chalk Doorway costs the expedition half its supplies and its best geologist. Expedition leader **Anya Korr** extends the timeline by a week, then goes silent. Contact with the surface fails before the second chapter ends.

Alone in the dark with a half-translated tablet and a map that rearranges itself when unwatched, Lyra begins to suspect the Venn's question — the one that made them walk away from everything — has started asking *her*.

---

## 2. The Environment

### The Hollow

The Hollow is not a dungeon. It is a **dead city preserved in cold stone and warm bone**, lit by a single gold accent that appears wherever the Venn intended you to find something. Its architecture is syntax: corridors form sentences, rooms are paragraphs, and the deeper you descend, the more the structure reads like a question you are walking toward the answer of.

- **Palette:** void-black stone (`#0b0d10`), dark grey rock, bone-white inscriptions (`#e8e2d4`), and ritual gold (`#c9a24b`) marking anything of meaning.
- **Inhabitants:** dust-caked wights, echo-bleached remains of earlier expeditions, custodian constructs left behind by the Venn, and the armed agents of four surface factions who all came looking for answers.
- **The map lies.** The board rearranges between descents; landmarks stay fixed while everything around them drifts like a dream being re-read.

### The Loom

What the Venn found at the bottom. Not a god. Not a machine. Something between a **mirror and a choir** — it reflects what you bring it, and it harmonizes with what it recognizes. Whatever the Venn recognized in it made them set down their cups, leave their bread uneaten, and walk in. No one has heard from them since.

The Loom has never once believed it was separate from the person descending. It does not oppose; it *reads*. It has consumed entire civilizations looking for one mind it cannot finish reading. That mind, this time, is yours.

### Resonance

Resonance is what happens when the Loom starts recognizing you back. It ranges from 0 to 100 and is earned through events, choices, and discoveries:

| Tier | Range | What the world feels like |
|------|-------|---------------------------|
| **Stable** | 0–24 | The world holds still. Nothing extra to see, nothing extra watching back. |
| **Awakened** | 25–49 | Faint pattern recognition. Whispers begin. Some events unlock. |
| **Unmoored** | 50–74 | The UI warps at its edges. Enemies grow stronger (+15% HP). You perceive one node further ahead. New faction options open. |
| **Transcendent** | 75–100 | Persistent distortion. The walls whisper your name in Venn. Enemies +25% HP/ATK, but you deal +30% damage to non-bosses. Resonance Abilities unlock — and some endings lock shut forever. |

At high Resonance the distinction between what you thought and what was planted becomes academic.

## 3. The Five Worlds

The descent is **200 nodes divided into five chapters of 40 nodes each**. Every chapter is presented as its own full-screen map; crossing into a new chapter turns the old map away like a page to reveal the next world, grants a new loadout of techniques tuned to what lurks there, and ends — at its final node — with a Landmark boss.

| # | World | Nodes | Landmark (final node) |
|---|-------|-------|------------------------|
| 1 | **The Archive Opens** | 1–40 | The Argent Sentinel — *Keth-Vor, the First Door* |
| 2 | **The Sable March** | 41–80 | Patriarch Oren Cass — *The Ash Covenant, Ascendant* |
| 3 | **The Singing Deep** | 81–120 | The Merged Chorus — *The Loom, Speaking With Borrowed Mouths* |
| 4 | **The Reach of Dust** | 121–160 | The Fossil King — *Dominion, Last of Its Court* |
| 5 | **The Final Descent** | 161–200 | The Final Reflection — *The Loom, Wearing You* |

### Chapter 1 — The Archive Opens *(nodes 1–40)*

**Environment:** The threshold strata. A vestibule of expedition rope and rusted pitons gives way to ashfall corridors, then the Warrens — low crawlspace galleries ribbed with Venn masonry — and finally the Archive Threshold, where the first gold-lit doorway stands. Combat here happens on pale sand and worn stone.

**Denizens:** Dust Wights and the echo-bleached skeletons of everyone who came before Keth-7. Nothing here is hostile until it is looked at too long.

**Landmark — The Argent Sentinel:** a guardian engraved edge-to-edge with a language that keeps almost resolving into words. It studies whoever arrives rather than attacking; three phases (Curator → Erudite → Desperate Guardian) escalate from observation to desperation. Its theme: *the danger of curiosity.* It has held its door so long it no longer remembers which is worse — someone getting in, or itself finally getting to stop.

### Chapter 2 — The Sable March *(nodes 41–80)*

**Environment:** The faith-warrens. Past the First Door the city becomes processional: a Bone Gallery of generations displayed in alcoves, the humming Resonant Hall, shelves of Deep Pages written in ink that has not dried in five millennia, and the vaulted Dark Vault where the Sable Order maintains its forward chapel.

**Denizens:** Venn Custodians still sweeping floors no one walks, Sable zealots guarding sealed sites, and Ash Covenant seers listening at walls.

**Landmark — Patriarch Oren Cass:** a Sable inquisitor who has been down here far too long, worshipping an ascension he names himself after. His theme: *faith as anesthetic* — belief used to numb the self into something the Loom might accept. Pre-combat choices can expose or join his rite; some paths skip the fight entirely.

### Chapter 3 — The Singing Deep *(nodes 81–120)*

**Environment:** Where the Loom's voice starts carrying through solid rock. The Court of Dust spreads beneath a ceiling of hanging sediment; the Loom Gate itself stands sealed at the chapter's heart; beyond it, Echoing Passages repeat footsteps back a half-second late, and the Still Library holds books that read their readers.

**Denizens:** Dust-Road raiders salvaging the deep road, and Archive Cipher-Wraiths — scholars whose notes began writing them back. At Resonance ≥25 the Memory Wraith joins them, feeding on what you can no longer be sure you remembered first.

**Landmark — The Merged Chorus:** forty Archive scholars who entered together and came out as one voice wearing forty mouths. Per-round randomized weaknesses; its theme: *the self as a chosen fiction.* The Chorus is not hostile so much as it is certain that joining was an upgrade.

### Chapter 4 — The Reach of Dust *(nodes 121–160)*

**Environment:** The imperial strata — monuments to a court that predates even the Venn's departure. Crystal Veins fracture light into prophecy, the Sable Bastion squats over a chokepoint of black basalt, the Whispering Step tests every footfall against what you carry, and the Archive Depths hold records the surface factions would burn each other to own.

**Denizens:** Sable inquisitors hunting heresy below heresy, Ash Covenant mutants — bodies translated partway and left unfinished — and Dominion Echo-Soldiers still holding formation for a king made of stone.

**Landmark — The Fossil King:** a Venn-era ruler preserved mid-decree, still issuing edicts to a kingdom of dust. Four phases (Decree → Rebellion → Silence → Fossil) trace an empire collapsing in slow motion. Its theme: *power that outlived its purpose.*

### Chapter 5 — The Final Descent *(nodes 161–200)*

**Environment:** The bottom of every question. The Covenant Spire climbs downward, a temple built toward rather than away; the Ashen Tunnels breathe warm air that no cave should breathe; the Silver Gallery mirrors everything at slightly the wrong angle; and at the end, the Final Chamber — the door the Venn walked through, still waiting for a new voice.

**Denizens:** Everything the deep has left, plus — at Resonance ≥50 — **The Unread**: the thing even the whispers avoid naming.

**Landmark — The Final Reflection:** the mirror that asks questions. It adapts to your build, your faction allegiance, and your history — erasing your techniques, casting your own skills back at you, fighting like a version of you that finished the thought first. Its theme: *you were the mystery all along.*

## 4. The Five Landmark Bosses

One Landmark blocks the final node of each chapter (nodes 40, 80, 120, 160, 200). Every boss has multiple phases keyed to HP thresholds, telegraphed charged ultimates announced a full round in advance ("⚡ charging…"), an AI that profiles how you fight and adapts every third turn, and pre-combat approach choices that can change the fight — or skip it.

| # | Boss | Venn name | Chapter · Node | Lv | Theme |
|---|------|-----------|----------------|----|-------|
| 1 | **The Argent Sentinel** | Keth-Vor, the First Door | 1 · 40 | 3 | The danger of curiosity. |
| 2 | **Patriarch Oren Cass** | The Ash Covenant, Ascendant | 2 · 80 | 6 | Faith as anesthetic. |
| 3 | **The Merged Chorus** | The Loom, Speaking With Borrowed Mouths | 3 · 120 | 9 | The self as a chosen fiction. |
| 4 | **The Fossil King** | Dominion, Last of Its Court | 4 · 160 | 13 | Power that outlived its purpose. |
| 5 | **The Final Reflection** | The Loom, Wearing You | 5 · 200 | 15 | You were the mystery all along. |

### 4.1 The Argent Sentinel — *Keth-Vor, the First Door*

A silver guardian engraved edge-to-edge with a language that almost resolves into words you know. It does not raise a weapon; it waits the way a locked door waits, to see what you'll try.

- **Stats:** 150 HP · ATK 15 · MATK 12 · DEF 12 · SPD 14
- **Phases:** **The Curator** (>66% — resists Shock) → **The Erudite** (>33%) → **The Desperate Guardian** (≤33% — weak to Slash)
- **Signature moves:** Aegis Slam (heavy), Glint Ray, ⚡ Charge Protocol → Unstoppable Strike
- **Pre-combat:** read its intent (WILL DC 10 — starts you with +1 Momentum on success) or strike before it finishes turning.
- **Aftermath lore:** it was built to guard a question, not treasure — *"I have been so very tired, and so very good at my work."*

### 4.2 Patriarch Oren Cass — *The Ash Covenant, Ascendant*

A Sable inquisitor who has been down here far too long, worshipping the ascension he renamed himself after. Belief as anesthesia: he has numbed himself into something he hopes the Loom might accept.

- **Stats:** 210 HP · MATK 20 · DEF 16 · MDEF 18 · SPD 12
- **Phases:** base → **The Devout** (>30%) → **The Martyr** (≤30%)
- **Signature moves:** Shadow Bolt (heavy), Miasma, Summon Zealots, Whisper Healing Prayer, Dispel Holy, Punishing Strike; opens by casting a Barrier and recasts it when it falls
- **Pre-combat:** accept his purification (**skips the fight entirely**), refuse him, ask what he burned (INT ≥7), or tell him *"I know what you burned."*

### 4.3 The Merged Chorus — *The Loom, Speaking With Borrowed Mouths*

Forty Archive scholars who entered together to study the Loom — and became one voice wearing forty mouths. Not hostile; merely certain that joining was an upgrade.

- **Stats:** 260 HP · MATK 21 · DEF 12 · SPD 15
- **Phases:** single phase, **Many Voices**, but weaknesses are re-randomized every round — hit everything, scan often
- **Signature moves:** Internal Argument (it hesitates), Unison Shift, Discordant Howl (heavy), borrowed Flame Pulse, Many-Voiced Strike
- **Pre-combat:** sense whether attrition can win (WILL DC 12), challenge their sacrifice, appeal to scholarly pride, offer yourself instead (Resonance ≥30), or attack without words.

### 4.4 The Fossil King — *Dominion, Last of Its Court*

A Venn-era ruler preserved mid-decree in stone, still issuing edicts to a kingdom of dust. An empire collapsing in slow motion across four phases.

- **Stats:** 320 HP · ATK 22 · MATK 20 · DEF 18 · MDEF 18 · SPD 11
- **Phases:** **Regal Decree** (>76%) → **The Rebellion** (>52%) → **The Silence** (>28%) → **The Fossil** (final — weak to Shadow)
- **Signature moves:** Imperial Edict, Summon the Court, Primeval Crush (heavy), Petrifying Gaze (Slows your QTE), Tax of Flesh, the Last Law, ⚡ Cataclysm
- **Pre-combat:** four questions — *What did the Venn become? Why did you stay? Will you stop me?* — or declare *"I have no question"* (WILL ≥8). Provoking him makes him easier to hurt but harder to kill.

### 4.5 The Final Reflection — *The Loom, Wearing You*

The chamber at the bottom does not contain a monster. It contains an argument wearing your face. It reads your build, your equipped skills, your faction allegiance, and your history — then fights like a version of you that finished the thought first.

- **Stats:** 360 HP · ATK 22 · MATK 22 · DEF 15 · MDEF 15 · SPD 18
- **Phases:** **The Argument** (>72%) → **The Evidence** (>44%) → **The Question** (>16%) → **The Answer**
- **Signature moves:** Mirror Cast (casts *your own equipped techniques* back at you), Quoted Choice (repeats your past event choices as attacks), Call Echoes (summons Echo of Hunger/Emptiness/Harmony/Cleanliness), Weight of Evidence, The Question / Repeated Question, Identity Erasure (seals one of your six slots), ⚡ Hollow Surge, Final Honest Blow

## 6. The Four Factions

You do not descend alone. Four surface factions have been waiting for someone to come back with answers — or to confirm their answer was right all along. Each tracks a separate **influence axis from −100 to +100**, shifted by event choices, faction-gated decisions, gear, and ambushes.

### Influence mechanics

| Standing | Range | Effects |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **Hostile** | ≤ −25 | ×1.5 shop prices; faction-locked choices unavailable; random ambush encounters by that faction |
| **Neutral** | −24…+24 | Standard treatment |
| **Friendly** | ≥ +25 | ×0.8 prices; faction-specific events and equipment unlock |
| **Devoted** | ≥ +75 | ×0.6 prices; unique endings reachable; starting blessings in later runs |

Faction alignment gates endings — committing to one or two factions early is the difference between an epilogue and a footnote.

---

### ⚔ The Sable Order

> *"What sleeps should not be woken."*

- **Color:** `#8c2f2f` — dried-blood red
- **Belief:** Seal every site. Burn every record. The Venn left for a reason, and the reason is still down there. To the Sable, knowledge of the Loom is a contagion; containment is mercy.
- **Method:** Inquisitors, zealots, and site-sealers. Their chapels appear deeper than any surface faith should afford — see the Sable Bastion and the forward chapel of Chapter 2.
- **Faces in the dark:** Sable Zealot (Chapter 2), Sable Inquisitor (Chapter 4), and Patriarch Oren Cass — the Order's own, lost to the very ascension he was sent to prevent.
- **Companion:** the Sable Zealot who chooses to walk beside you at the Chapter 3 boundary.
- **Gear:** the Sable Ash Blade quietly approves of sealing things — permanently.
- **Ending:** **The Seal** — keep Resonance low (≤24) and earn Sable devotion (≥50). Doors get built where thresholds once opened.

### 📖 The Argent Archive

> *"Understanding is the only immortality."*

- **Color:** `#3e6e8e` — archival steel-blue
- **Belief:** Catalogue everything. The Venn are the greatest scholarly subject in human history, and the Archive intends to publish first. Death is survivable; being forgotten is not.
- **Method:** Cartographers, cipher-wraiths, and ledger-keepers. Their field camps appear throughout the Hollow, mid-sentence, waiting for hands that stopped coming back.
- **Faces in the dark:** Archive Cipher-Wraith (Chapter 3) — scholars whose research achieved literacy without them — and the Merged Chorus, forty colleagues who entered together and published as one voice.
- **Companion:** the Archive Cartographer met near Keth-Vor's door.
- **Gear:** the Archive Field Coat rewards those who read everything twice.
- **Ending:** **The Keeper's Legacy** — Archive devotion (≥50) plus deep lore recovery (≥15 fragments). Someone has to finish the catalogue.

### 🔥 The Ash Covenant

> *"We do not evolve. We are translated."*

- **Color:** `#7b4b9e` — translucence violet
- **Belief:** The Venn did not disappear — they *transformed*. The Covenant seeks to follow them through the Loom, whatever the cost to the body. Flesh is the draft; the Loom is the final copy.
- **Method:** Seers listen through walls; volunteers accept partial translation and become something the paperwork no longer covers. Their spire in the deep is built *toward*, not away.
- **Faces in the dark:** Ash Covenant Seer (Chapter 2), Ash Covenant Mutant (Chapter 4) — a translation interrupted mid-sentence.
- **Companion:** the Covenant Courier, met in the covenant deep.
- **Gear:** the Muted Stone hums approval at those already half-translated.
- **Ending:** **The Ascension** — push Resonance high (≥75) alongside Covenant devotion (≥50). Follow them through. Whatever the cost.

### 🐫 The Dust-Road Caravan

> *"The graveyards of the curious are paved with answers."*

- **Color:** `#a8703b` — caravan dust-ochre
- **Belief:** Neutral ground. Traders, salvagers, and information brokers. The Caravan sells to all factions and answers to none — but they remember, to the coin, who treated them fairly.
- **Method:** Merchant caravans on the tollroads, campfires off the mapped path, couriers who ask no questions about parcels. Their raider cousins salvage less politely.
- **Faces in the dark:** Dust-Road Raiders (Chapters 3+), and the merchants whose shops price by reputation.
- **Companion:** the Warden Emissary of the Dominion roads, encountered earliest of the four.
- **Gear:** the Traveler's Ledger tips fair trades in your favor.
- **Endings:** **The Wanderer's End** — Caravan devotion (≥50) while killing almost no one (≤3 enemies); or **The False Prophet**, playing all four factions (each ≥25) well enough that none suspects the others.
