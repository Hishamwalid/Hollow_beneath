# THE HOLLOW BENEATH

*Five thousand years ago, the Venn walked into the dark and did not return. You are about to find out why.*

---

## Story

For seven hundred years, certain people have shared the same dream: an impossible version of themselves, power without limit, the promise of becoming everything they could be. They are called **Seekers**, and they descend into the Beneath chasing it. None of them come back.

You are not a Seeker. You never dreamed the dream.

Eve was one. She went down, came back wrong, and forgot how to breathe when you were twenty-one. She left you a house with dust on every surface except one desk, a leather journal, and a painted portrait of herself smiling at the mouth of a cave. Now a company of delvers has gone silent at **the Delving of Keth** — and the voice waiting in the dark below sounds exactly like her.

You take up the journal and walk toward the sinkhole for one reason:

**You want to understand what happened to your mother.**

---

## The World

**The Beneath** is not a dungeon. It is a dead city preserved in cold stone and warm bone, lit by a single gold accent that appears wherever the Venn intended you to find something. Its architecture is syntax — corridors form sentences, rooms are paragraphs, and the deeper you go, the more the structure reads like a question you are walking toward the answer of.

**The Loom** is what the Venn found at the bottom. Not a god. Not a machine. Something between a mirror and a choir — it reflects what you bring it, and it harmonizes with what it recognizes. It does not destroy identity; it *perfects* it. Whoever reaches the end and finishes the thought becomes part of **the Hollow** — the thing that keeps the door open, and the promise alive, for whoever dreams next.

**Resonance** is what happens when the Loom starts recognizing *you* back. Low Resonance feels like intuition. High Resonance feels like possession. At Transcendent levels (75+), the walls whisper your name in Venn. The distinction between what you thought and what was planted stops mattering.

## The Four Factions

You do not descend alone. Four surface factions have been waiting for someone to come back with answers — or to confirm their answer was right all along.

| Faction | Motto | Belief |
|---------|-------|--------|
| **The Sable Order** | *What sleeps should not be woken.* | Seal every site. Burn every record. The Venn left for a reason, and the reason is still down there. |
| **The Argent Archive** | *Understanding is the only immortality.* | Catalogue everything. The Venn are the greatest scholarly subject in mortal memory, and the Archive intends to be the first to set the account down. |
| **The Ash Covenant** | *We do not change. We are translated.* | The Venn did not disappear — they *transformed*. The Covenant seeks to follow them through the Loom, whatever the cost to the body. |
| **The Dust-Road Caravan** | *The graveyards of the curious are paved with answers.* | Neutral ground. Traders, salvagers, rumor-brokers. The Caravan sells to all factions and answers to none — but they remember who treated them fairly. |

## The Five Bosses

The board is 200 nodes long. At nodes 40, 80, 120, 160, and 200 (the final node of chapters 1 through 5), a Landmark blocks your path — each one a major boss with unique mechanics, phase transitions, and lore.

| Boss | Node | Theme |
|------|------|-------|
| **The Argent Sentinel** (Keth-Vor, the First Door) | 40 | *The danger of curiosity.* — A guardian that studies you. Three phases: Curator, Erudite, Desperate Guardian. |
| **Patriarch Oren Cass** | 80 | *Faith as anesthetic.* — A Sable inquisitor who has been down here too long. Pre-combat choices that can skip the fight entirely. |
| **The Merged Chorus** | 120 | *The cost of not being alone.* — Forty Archive scholars who entered together and became one voice. Per-round randomized weakness. |
| **The Fossil King** (Dominion, Last of Its Court) | 160 | *Power that outlived its purpose.* — A ruler petrified mid-decree. Four phases: Decree, Rebellion, Silence, Fossil. |
| **The Final Reflection** | 200 | *The mirror that asks questions.* — Adapts to your build, your faction alignment, your history. Fights like a version of you. |

## The Endings

Definitive edition: **three witnessed fates, plus one the Loom only shows those who already know what it sounds like when it stops.** Outcome flags decide everything (evaluated top-to-bottom); faction allegiance colors only the closing epilogue.

| Ending | Trigger |
|--------|---------|
| **THE HOLLOW** | Defeat the Final Reflection — you become the next Hollow. |
| **LOST IN THE DARK** | Fall to the Reflection and accept the dark. |
| **THE RETURN** | Fall to the Reflection and climb home — where forgetting waits. |
| **THE SILENCE** *(hidden)* | New Game+ · have silenced the Loom twice · reach Transcendent resonance. Unask the question itself. |

Ending texts braid in what your run actually did: the mother's journal, the marked child, the page you wrote "for the next one," the mercy you showed a broken soldier.

## Core Game Mechanics

### Stats & Character Creation

You have 5 stats, allocated from a pool of 30 points (minimum 1, maximum 10 per stat):

| Stat | Governs | Derived bonus |
|------|---------|---------------|
| **STR** | Physical attack power | ATK = STR × 2 |
| **DEX** | Speed, accuracy, dodge | SPD = DEX × 2 + 8, ACC = 80 + DEX × 2 (max 95), Dodge = DEX × 2 (max 40) |
| **CON** | Hit points, physical defense | HP = CON × 10 + 30, DEF = CON × 2 |
| **INT** | Magic attack, magic defense | MATK = INT × 2, MDEF = INT × 2 + WIL |
| **WIL** | Magic points, magic defense | MP = WIL × 6 + 20, also contributes to MDEF |

Six preset builds available: **Balanced** (6/6/6/6/6), **Warrior** (9/5/8/2/6), **Scholar** (2/5/4/9/10), **Ranger** (5/10/4/4/7), **Guardian** (6/4/10/3/7), **Shadow** (4/7/5/6/8).

### Loadout (6-Slot Skill System)

There are no classes. Every player is a lone protagonist with the same potential; what defines you is what you choose to carry.

- At descent you know the **Chapter 1 loadout** (`Cleaving Swing`, `Pinpoint Strike`, `Flame Pulse`, `Heavy Guard`, `Mend`, `Ignite`).
- Crossing into a new chapter **grants its techniques** outright — chapter 2 unlocks `Frost Touch`/`Shock Arc`/`Cleanse & Surge`, chapter 3 brings `Chain Lightning`/`Inferno Wave`/`Sacred Ray`/`Aegis Ward`, and so on through `Winter's Grasp`/`Aegis Covenant` in the deep. Bosses teach their own arts (`Steady Hands`, `Martyr's Flame`); discoveries hide more.
- At Transcendent Resonance the Loom itself teaches you **Loom Lance** and **Echo Ward**.
- Six slots are **active in combat**; everything else waits in your **Archive** and can be swapped freely between fights. No skill points, no trees — new chapters and discoveries feed the pool directly.

### Board Movement

200 nodes arranged across **5 chapters** of 40 nodes each. Every chapter is its own full-screen map; finishing one turns away like a page to reveal the next. Each turn:
- **Roll 1d6** — advance that many nodes forward (cannot skip unresolved Landmark nodes).
- Each node type triggers different content:

| Node | Weight | What happens |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Event** | 45% | Narrative encounter with 2–4 choices, stat checks, faction shifts, item/lore rewards |
| **Combat** | 22% | Turn-based fight (1 enemy early; pairs appear in chapters 3+) |
| **Rest** | 12% | Restore HP/MP, remove status effects |
| **Discovery** | 13% | Random reward: gold, item, lore fragment, Echo Shards, faction influence, or skill |
| **Trap** | 8% | Triggers a hazard (collapsing floor, still-water reflection, etc.) — roll to avoid or suffer |
| **Landmark** | 5 fixed | Major boss encounter at nodes 40/80/120/160/200 |

Chapter 1 is human country — Keth deserters, rust-pickers, Sable Scouts — the armed leftovers of the dig. The deeper dead wake up later. Enemies scale with depth: +10% HP, +7.5% ATK, +5% DEF for every 10 nodes descended (continuous, multiplicative from base).

### Combat System ("Echo" — see `docs/COMBAT_SYSTEM_REVAMP.md`)

Solo turn-based — **one action per turn** then `END TURN`; enemies answer in speed order. Enemy affinities start **unknown (`?`)** for all eight types:

| Type | Color | Type | Color |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| Slash | Red | Flame | Orange |
| Pierce | Gold | Frost | Blue |
| Blunt | Brown | Shock | Purple |
| Sacred | White | Shadow | Grey |

Hit a foe with a damage type to **discover that slot forever** (`wk` weakness / `str` resist / `null` nullify / `rep` reflect / `drn` drain / `-` neutral — persisted per enemy id in your Bestiary across runs). Every discovery is also available as a free **Scan** showing name/Lv, discovered affinity chips, and the move list.

| Action | Cost | What it does |
|--------|------|--------------|
| **Attack** | — | Basic Slash/Pierce. Lands instantly — no timing needed. |
| **Skill** | MP or HP | One of your six equipped techniques. Offensive skills run a **QTE needle** — PERFECT (+30% dmg, big crit bump), good, or miss (×0.8). `Slowed` doubles needle speed. |
| **Guard** | — | Halve incoming damage, **recover +6 MP**, prevent Down/Stagger from enemy crits. |
| **Scan** | Free | Inspect the selected enemy — affinities found so far, moves, telegraphs. |
| **Item** | Consumable | Use an inventory item (heals, cures). |
| **End Turn** | — | Pass — enemies take their turn. |

**Damage:** `max(3, round((ATK or MATK − DEF/2) × affinity-mult))` with ±10% variance; `wk` ×1.5, `str` ×0.5, `null` 0, `rep` bounces back at you, `drn` heals the foe; crit ×1.5 on top.

**Weakness consequence — Down + 1-More:** landing `wk` (or any crit) **Downs** the target (they lose their next turn) and grants you **1-More** — an immediate extra action.

**Reactions:** elements left on a foe detonate under the right follow-up — `Chilled→Shock` **Brittle Frost** (stun, ×1.25), `Shock DoT→Flame` **Overcharge** (×1.3 — Shock Arc and Chain Lightning leave the charge), `Sacred-mark→Shadow` **Eclipse** (strips buffs, ×2).

**Living opposition:** adaptive bosses read your favorite element every third round and resist it for two ("IT ADAPTS!"). Charged ultimates announce `⚡ CHARGING` a round early. Deep fights roll **battlefield weather** — Dust Storm (−10% accuracy) or Sacred Ground (Sacred +20%). Cornered blows land harder: once per fight, striking from under 25% HP is a **Desperate Gambit** (×1.5).

**Momentum payoffs** — the gauge banks between fights; at 5, choose one:

| Choice | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Flow** | Act again now; start next round exhausted (−25% damage) |
| **Harmony** | Heal 25% max HP; bosses enrage (+30%, 2 turns) |
| **Archive** | Reveal the current boss phase |
| **Forgotten Technique** | Next skill costs 0 MP |
| **Unravel** | Next hit ×2.5 damage, ignores 75% DEF |
| **Echo Surge** | All damage +20% for 3 turns |
| **Phase Shift** | Dodge the next 2 attacks |
| **Desperate Strike** | All attacks crit this turn |
| **Overclock** | +70% damage this turn; lose 20% max HP |

### Resonance

The central mechanic. Earned through events, choices, and discoveries; spent by some rites and quiet moments. Ranges 0–100.

| Tier | Threshold | Effects |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Stable** | 0–24 | Nothing extra watching you. |
| **Awakened** | 25–49 | Whispers begin. Some events unlock. Memory Wraiths stir (ch3+). |
| **Unmoored** | 50–74 | UI warps at edges. Enemies +15% HP. One node further ahead. The Unread hunts (ch5). |
| **Transcendent** | 75–100 | Enemies +25% HP/ATK; you deal +30% to non-bosses. **The Loom teaches you Loom Lance & Echo Ward.** |

### Faction Influence

Four axes from −100 (Hostile) to +100 (Devoted):

| Standing | What it means |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Hostile (−100…−25) | Faction-locked choices unavailable; 30% ambush chance while traveling; rest disruptions |
| Neutral (−24…24) | Standard options |
| Friendly (25…74) | Faction-specific positive events and equipment |
| Devoted (75…100) | Unique perks (rest boons, discovery bonuses), each faction's thank-you scene, and the closing epilogue |

### Difficulty

Selectable in Settings, effective immediately:

| Mode | Player dmg | Incoming dmg | Enemy HP/ATK/DEF | Permadeath |
|------|-----------|--------------|------------------|------------|
| **Easy** | ×1.2 | ×0.85 | ×0.80 / ×0.85 / ×0.85 | No |
| **Normal** | ×1 | ×1 | ×1 | No |
| **Hard** | ×0.9 | ×1.15 | ×1.20 / ×1.12 / ×1.10 | No |
| **Ironman** | ×0.85 | ×1.30 | ×1.30 / ×1.18 / ×1.15 | Yes — death ends the run |

### Companions

None. The descent is walked alone — by design.

### Progression

- **XP** from victories and events; per-level cost grows (`25 + 7n + 0.35n²` for level n→n+1), stretching the curve across the whole descent.
- **Level cap:** 15 — one stat point each. Skills come from chapter loadouts and discoveries, never level-ups.
- **~43 named techniques**: five chapter grants, discoverables, boss trophies, two Resonance Abilities.
- **Equipment:** 4 slots (Weapon, Armour, Focus, Accessory), bonuses applied on equip.

### Echo Shards

Meta-currency earned across runs (per node/boss/ending/lore-fragment; death refunds 50%). **11 permanent unlocks** in the Shard Shop — from Rusty Dagger+ (50 shards) to **New Game+** (1000): enemies +25%, the Beneath remembers, and THE SILENCE becomes possible. Purchases persist forever.

In-run, the board's **Shard Rites** panel spends run-held shards on rest-healing, calming Resonance, or faction favor.

### Stat Checks

```
Roll 1d20 + StatValue × 2 ≥ DC + 10
```
At stat 6 this passes DC 10 ~65% and DC 16 ~35%; investing 9–10 raises DC 16 success to ~85%.

## How to Play

### Starting a Run

1. **Main Menu** — "New Descent" (or "Continue" if a descent is active).
2. **Character Creation** — preset build or manual allocation.
3. **Tutorial** — first-timers get the Delver's Primer. Skippable.
4. **Descend.**

### Each Turn

1. Click **Roll** — your token hops that many nodes along the path.
2. The destination resolves: combat, event, rest, discovery, trap, or Landmark boss.
3. The **Journal** keeps your whole run's history (scroll it); the beat chip keeps the current story thread visible.
4. Between rolls: Inventory, Loadout (six-slot swap), Lore Codex, Shard Rites, Settings.

### Tips for New Players

- **Resonance is a double-edged sword.** It unlocks abilities and content, and it makes everything down there meaner.
- **Factions don't pick your ending — your flags do.** But Devoted factions earn scenes, perks, and the epilogue's last word. Commit early.
- **Hit every affinity at least once.** `wk` Downs the foe and grants 1-More. The Bestiary remembers across descents.
- **Guard is an economy.** Halves damage, blocks Stagger, banks +6 MP.
- **Reactions win long fights.** Chilled then Shocked stuns; Shock DoT into Inferno Wave overcharges; mark Sacred, cut with Shadow.
- **Bosses learn.** If one starts resisting your favorite element, it read your habits — switch answers.
- **Six slots is a puzzle.** Re-slot from Loadout between fights for the next stage's weaknesses.
- **Checkpoints catch you** at nodes 40/80/120/160 — death continues there at half HP/MP. Ironman doesn't.
